The pod was removed by the HPA, not by a probe or a crash. It looks like a replica-count fight between the HPA and the Deployment, which has been repeating all hour.

**Evidence**
- The current pods are `emailservice-7959ddb655-s98dz` (1h old) and `-c9txp` (2m old). Both have 0 restarts and are Ready 1/1, so nothing is crash-looping.
- The events show the same cycle many times, about every 5 minutes (c5v9n, h5p88, zn8tx, snnf8, 8rhk2 and others):
  - The Deployment is "Scaled up … from 1 to 2" (×20).
  - The HPA reports `SuccessfulRescale … New size: 1; reason: All metrics below target` (×19).
  - The Deployment is "Scaled down … from 2 to 1" (×19).
  - The ReplicaSet then logs `SuccessfulDelete`, and the kubelet logs `Killing … Stopping container server` on the removed pod.
- Replacing `-c5v9n` 2m ago was part of this cycle. A controller deleted it.
- The `Readiness probe failed … timeout … within 1s` warnings appear on every new pod shortly after it starts. A readiness failure only removes a pod from Service endpoints, so it did not cause the deletions.
- The Deployment's generation is 40 (the other services are at 2). That is consistent with its replica count being changed about 40 times.
- The emailservice HPA is `min 1, max 3` at 1% CPU against a 70% target. Every other service's HPA has min 2.
- The emailservice Deployment currently shows desired 2.
- The image tag is the same on every pod (`v20261004-134739-d34db63e`). The last commit to `values-dev.yaml` (f124e613, 13:51) only changed the image tag. No rollout caused this.

**Likely cause**
Git sets emailservice to 2 replicas, while its HPA allows a minimum of 1. With CPU near idle, the HPA scales down to 1. Argo CD self-heal then resets the replica count to 2, and the HPA scales it down again. Each cycle deletes a pod and creates a new one, which is why one pod is always young.

I did not see the emailservice `replicas` line in `values.yaml`, because the file read was truncated. That makes the Git-side value an inference from the Deployment showing desired 2. I also did not check Argo CD's own state.

**Fix (through Git)**
Align the two settings, for example `replicas: 1` with the HPA at min 1, or HPA `minReplicas: 2` like the other services.

**Separate issue**
The readiness probe has a 1s timeout and fails twice on every new pod. This is worth tuning with `timeoutSeconds` or `initialDelaySeconds`.
